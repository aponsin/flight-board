- 2026-11-16: A card is not read as live while a commit cited in its next_note is not on main (plan-part-on-main, #C890, half 2 of #C863; the #C731 case). `collector/state.mjs` `citedShas(note)`: only a sha marked by the word `part` ("part 1a2b3c4", whitespace between) counts; every other sha in a note (a superseded tip, a review tip, an old stack) is ignored. The marked word is 7 to 40 lower-case hex characters that has a digit and a letter a to f and stands alone (not inside a longer word or number), at most 10, in the note's order. In `tipState`, a card that would read `live` with any cited sha that is not an ancestor of the repository's main (`git merge-base --is-ancestor`; a sha the repository cannot find counts as not on main) reads `ship` instead, with `partsMissing` (the missing shas as written) on its reading and live left out of `seen`. `collector/state-schema.mjs`: `partsMissing` is an optional card field, a list of 1 to 10 distinct 7-to-40-character hex words. A note with no "part <sha>" marker reads as before (an unmarked sha holds nothing); a card not at live is not touched. A sha of seven digits and no letter is not read as a sha (nor is a long number or a date). Probe only (no Worker, page or migration change). Tests: `tests/plan-part-on-main.test.mjs`.
