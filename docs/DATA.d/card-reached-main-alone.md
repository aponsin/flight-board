- 2026-11-17: Only a group's head lands a group (#C1138). `landHoldsFile` (`collector/store-dispatch.mjs`) adds `groups` to `land-holds.json`: for each card of a group, the other members of that group still to land (open, not dropped or closed, and neither live nor on main); a card with no group, or none left beside it, is not listed. `landPlan` (`scripts/board-land.mjs`) now waits a tip that lacks any of them ("its group is not whole: this tip lacks ..."), so a single signed card's tip is never tried while its group has other members to land, and the group's head, which carries every one of them in its Board-Id trailers, is. A holds file with no `groups` (a probe from before this) asks nothing more. `cards` is unchanged. No migration, table or column. Ships as the owner's pack (the probe writes the new key; the land job runs from the signed link and takes it as soon as it is written).
