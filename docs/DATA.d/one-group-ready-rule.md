- 2026-11-20: One group stage and one rework rule (#C1391, the owner in t-1795228717929; his pick A on #T2255). DEFINITION CHANGE, three definitions: 1. **A card's stage.** `stageOf(card, evidence)` in `worker/group-rule.mjs` (which imports nothing) returns exactly one of, in order: `unbuilt`, `built`, `signed`, `landed`, `live`. `built`: its newest pushed head is declared ready and nothing sends it back: a head exists, it is not a start push (`wipHead`), the probe's `stackHold` is not set (in a stack repository: a `stack-ready` or `stack-ready-waiting` note is on that exact head), its reading is past build, and it is not in the owner's rework (below); anything less is `unbuilt`. `signed`: built, and its reading is at ship (every review it needs is signed on that head). `landed`: its commit is on main. `live`: its reading is live. 2. **A group's stage.** `groupStage(card, cards, evidenceOf, { need, repo, except })` is the lowest stage among its counted members: a group is its cards, as its tiles already are (#C1343, #C1402). The counted members are every card of the batch that is open (not dropped, not closed) and belongs to the asked repository; a card whose repository word is not known yet counts in every repository (it fails closed, #C1144). **A paused or blocked card counts.** A card in no group is a group of one. The function also returns who is behind: `waitsFor`, the members below the stage asked, each with its reason (not pushed, start push, no READY, sent back, paused, blocked). The group is **ready** (the one meaning of "group ready", and of the READY) when its stage is `built` or higher. To send a group on without one of its cards, the lead pulls that card out of the group (the existing pull-out); nothing else skips a member. What each reader asks, and nothing else: the reading (`waitOf`): a `built` card whose group is not ready has `wait` = `group`, `for` = `waitsFor`; the dispatch and the idle stall line (`openGroupWithheld`): a role starts no new card outside a started group that is not ready while one of that group's unbuilt cards is the role's turn (`ownerTurn`, not held), and when every card it waits for is paused, blocked or another role's, the role takes other work, as today; the slow line and the stuck state skip a card whose `wait` is `group`, the clock starting when the group became ready (the latest member's READY, `groupReadyAt`, #C1358); `stack-ready` writes the signal only when every other counted member is `built`, else the waiting note naming them, and the queue file (`stack-queue.json`) carries each card's `stage` (an old queue file with no stage gives the waiting note, never a signal); the landing: `land-holds.json` keeps its shape, a card's word is "group" while its group's stage is below `signed`, and `groups` lists the counted members below `landed`, both computed by `groupStage`. 3. **Rework.** `reworkKind` is the one rule and names three kinds: `findings` (a review's note on the card's head says FINDINGS while the card sits at that review's phase), `reason` (the store read a step back and no reason is attached yet: #C875, #C981), and `unexplained` (a step back from a review with no FINDINGS and no reason, #C1114). The first two are the owner's rework: the card is `unbuilt`, is with its owner, and goes first in the owner's queue. `unexplained` is the reviewer's turn and not the owner's rework: the card keeps its stage. A new head after sign-offs alone (a re-cut) is not rework; it is `unbuilt` until its READY is declared again, like any new head. This corrects the words "or a tip newer than its last sign-off" in #C1380's line and in the reading's `why`. Fixed with it: `stack-ready` no longer counts a groupmate's start push as built, a paused member is treated the same before the READY and at landing, and a member sent back with findings reads unbuilt to the cards stacked on it. `scripts/board-land.mjs` and its pinned import closure do not change; the rule lives in probe code (`collector/store-dispatch.mjs`, `worker/group-rule.mjs`), so it runs only after the owner installs the pack that re-pins the probe tree, as for any probe change, and until then the old rule runs whole.
