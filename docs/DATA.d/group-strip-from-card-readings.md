- 2026-11-20: A group's phase tile takes its class from its cards, and the pack a card names is the pack that carries it (#C1402; the owner's screenshot of #G119: the group's Land tile was yellow "pc123 · <owner>" while the three cards' were green with the same label). The class of a tile is chosen by one page function (`tileClass`) from the tile (`tileOf`/`tileAt`) and the card's reading (`wait`, its repository), and the card row and the group row both ask it; the group has no class rule of its own, takes the class its cards share at that phase, and the plain wait when they differ (an install wait and a pack wait are both "afterwork"), so a group is green only when every card with that tile is green. The one known difference stays: a card draws Pack and Install it has no evidence for as "unlit" and the group draws the plain future tile, whose title counts the cards (#C1343, #C1383). The reading's `pack` (the number, #C1382; `packOf` in collector/pairs.mjs, #C1309) used to be the OLDEST pair that holds the landing, ran or not, so a pack that never ran and was replaced by a newer one (pc123, replaced by pc124) kept the label; it is now the OLDEST holding pair that ran (the pack the card rode in), or, while none has run, the NEWEST holding pair (the one to install). `installed` is unchanged: any holding pair that ran, read from its `.done` marker. No new field, no migration. Ships as the page and a probe tree change (collector/pairs.mjs is pinned probe code: a hooks re-pin).
