- 2026-11-20: One reading per card, and every view draws it (#C1380, step 1 of 4: the module, its fixtures and its laws; no view calls it yet, so nothing on the board changes with this step, the owner in t-1795197124142: the card, its group and "Who is on what" must say the same thing; the owner's pick A on #T2194). DEFINITION CHANGE, four definitions: 1. **The reading.** `reading()` in `worker/reading.mjs` is the only code that says where a card is. It is pure: the card's row, the probe's evidence for it, the open review claim, the agents' readings, the config and the time go in. It returns `phase`, `state`, `wait`, `with`, `working`, `since`, `asOf` and `why`. The Worker's view carries it for every open card and every card closed in the last 7 days. No other file computes a phase, a state, a wait or who has a card: the dispatch, the stall lines, `stack-ready` and the landing job call the same module, and the page only draws. 2. **Card states** (replaces the "Card states" line of 2026-11-04). `state` is exactly one of the following, and the first that holds wins: - `done`: it read live. - `unknown`: a source the phase rests on could not be read and there is no earlier good reading of it (or the repository word is not in the config); `why` names it. A failed read that has an earlier good reading is not unknown: it is stale (below). - `rework`: FINDINGS on its tip, or a tip newer than its last sign-off. It is with its owner again, whatever phase is lit. A first push is never rework (#C981), and the reason rule of the Rework line is unchanged. - `working`: the role it is with has a session on it now, or CI or a ship run for it is going. - `waiting`: nobody is on it and `wait` says why. - `stuck`: nobody is on it, it has been at its phase for over twice the median, and no wait explains it (the Tile states line's own words). - `queued`: not started, no push of its own yet. - otherwise it is at its phase with nobody on it and not yet slow: `waiting` with `wait` = `turn`. `working` (the field) is true whenever a session is on the card, in `rework` too. Paused, Dropped, Blocked and Gated stay overlays, typed or from an open ask, as before. **Stalled is unchanged** (15 minutes at a phase while that phase's agent is idle; it wakes Manager once per reason): it is now asked of the reading (`working` false, `wait` none or `turn`, the `with` role idle), and the page's own "no step logged for 30 min" rule goes: the pill says Stalled when the stall rule says so. 3. **Waits.** `wait` is one of: `turn` (the phase's role is on another card), `group` (a groupmate is not built yet, #C1358), `land` (signed, waits for the landing or the ship), `pack` and `install` (a card of the board's own repository only: on main, not yet in a signed pack; in a pack the owner has not installed), `deploy` (the platform repository: on main, the deploy that carries it is not green yet), `ask` (an open ask or an unpicked decision, with its number, #C1294), `hold` (a hold with an end, #C1104). A card of the platform repository never has `pack` or `install`. 4. **Closed means live** (replaces `closesAtLanding` of #C1104 for the platform repository; the hold part of #C1104 is unchanged). A card closes when its reading is live, in every repository the config lists: the platform repository when its commit is inside the newest green deploy and a commit naming a version contains it (any commit on main's first-parent line that has the card's commit as an ancestor, so a commit inside a group head counts); the board's own repository when its commit is in the Worker's deployed commit and in the Studio's tree, or it changed only docs and tests. A card no longer closes at landing because it carries a repository word. Which repository a card belongs to is read from the config's repository list (`repoKind`: board or platform); a word the list does not know reads `unknown`, never board. A card with no commit of its own closes as it does today (typed done). Also, with no definition changed: - **With** is one role: the card's owner at plan, build and rework; at a review phase the role that holds the open claim on the tip, else the repository's reviewer from the config (#C1100; so the second checker is named as itself); the release role at land and ship; the owner of the board at `install` and `ask`. - **Tile looks** are the Tile states line's, unchanged (#C1267, #C1300), and each is chosen from `state` and `wait` only: `done` green; `working` blinking blue; `ask` red and white stripes; `install` green and white stripes; `turn`, `group`, `land`, `pack`, `deploy` and `hold` yellow and white stripes; `stuck` the dark tile; `unknown` the plain ring of #C1277, its hover naming the source that could not be read. Not needed, moving and future are drawn as today. The precedence of the Tile states line is the order of the states above. - **Groups.** A group's row is `groupReading(readings)`. Its rule is #C1343's, unchanged: per phase, the cards' tile when they agree, else the first state in `TILE_ORDER` that any card is in. What changes is where it reads: the members' readings, never the classes of a drawn tile, and nothing else. Its count is members done of members. - **Who is on what** (row 17, #C1331: the columns, the numbers, the looks and the dashed "work" badge are unchanged). "Every open card of the agent" means every open card whose `with` is that agent's role, so each open card is in exactly one column, and a card waiting at a review sits in that reviewer's column. Number 1 is the card the agent's session is on, when that card is one of them; the transcript's last mentioned card picks nothing else. A badge blinks only when `working` is true. - **Stale, unknown and age** (builds on #C1377, whose `stale` and `staleSince` stay as they are). A read that fails (git, GitHub, a pair folder, a session file) is never read as "no", and an old value never gets a new time. Two cases, never both: when there is an earlier good reading, the reading keeps that last good `state` and `wait`, carries `stale: true` and `staleSince`, and is drawn with #C1377's mark ("no fresh reading since HH:MM", never red), now on the card, its group row and its badge in "Who is on what" alike; when there is none, the state is `unknown`. `asOf` is the oldest source the reading rests on, so on a stale reading it equals `staleSince`. A stale reading is never `working` (no badge blinks on an old reading) and never closes a card: done needs a fresh read. - **Why.** One plain line for the hover, built from the same facts ("on main since 10:15; not in the installed tree; pack pc117 waits for the owner").
