- 2026-11-17: A card's `waiting_on` has one reading (#C1164, `worker/held-by.mjs` `isHeldBy`), read from one role's side (the card's owner by default): it holds the card when it has words and does not start with that role. "waiting on the owner" and "next is Checker" hold a Boardsmith card; "Checker: Full review" holds it for the owner but not for Checker; "Boardsmith: next" on a Boardsmith card is the owner's own pointer and holds nothing; empty holds nothing. Before, it was read five ways (the dispatch ignored it for the owner's build turn, `stalledWaits` and `slowWakes` took any words as a hold, the open-group rule and the next-card pick took it only when it did not start with the owner's role, the page took only the board owner's name, and `gate/reset.mjs` kept a sixth copy), so one card could be held on the stall line, woken by the dispatch and shown stalled on the page. The dispatch, `stalledWaits`, `slowWakes`, the open-group rule, the next-card pick and `gate/reset.mjs` all call `isHeldBy`/`heldFor`; the view carries `heldBy` (the words or absent) for the page, which keeps no rule of its own (`heldOnPurpose` and `stalled` read it). A hold's end is unchanged (check_at, hold_end, or the 2 h the store gives a waiting_on). No change to the Stalled definition: what is exempt is as before. One visible change: on the page a card held on any name other than its owner's (not only the board owner) keeps a plain row and is never shown stalled. Ships as a Worker deploy and a page publish, plus `gate/reset.mjs` and the new pure file for the next owner pack. Tests: `tests/waiting-on-one-reading.test.mjs`.
