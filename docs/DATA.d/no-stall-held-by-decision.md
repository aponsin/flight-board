- 2026-11-23: The "held by open decision <id>" stall row (#C1073: a card held by the lead's open decision whose owner has been idle for the stall time) is told ONCE per (card, decision id), not once per idle time (#C1412). Its words are now `#C<n> held by open decision <message id>, <owner> idle` with no "since <time>" (the time moved each time the owner ran a turn, so the same hold woke Manager again). The row stays while that decision holds the card, whatever the owner does meanwhile, and goes when the decision is answered, resolved, withdrawn or replaced; a new decision on the card, or a hold that ends and starts again, tells Manager again. A card held by an open decision is still out of the dispatch and the idle-agent count, and once the decision is answered an idle owner stalls it as a queued card as before.
