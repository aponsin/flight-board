- 2026-11-19: DEFINITION CHANGE, the stripe colours of the step tiles (#C1300, the owner's pick A on the stripe-colours thread; replaces the stripes part of #C1267 and #C1294). Yellow and white stripes are the normal process: an agent busy, another card or an unfinished group, a landed board card queued for its pack (the tile says "Queued for pack"), queued for a board release by Release, and a product ship. Red and white stripes are the owner: an open ask or an unpicked decision (unchanged). Green and white stripes mean one thing: the owner's install, for a card of the board's own repository whose pack is cut and waits for it (evidence `packed`, or the install wait the repository explains); a product card is never green. The install's own ask reads green, not red: an open gate at the phase that holds an install wait draws no red box. Before, a board release wait, a product ship and a landed card with no pack all read green. The Tile states row in DATA.md says the same; precedence is unchanged. Page only (page/board.template.html); no data, no Worker, no migration. Tests: `tests/tile-states-visible.test.mjs`, `tests/ship-cell-waits-for-install.test.mjs`.
