# Flight Board Data

The board's data contract: every data point the board shows, where it lives, who writes it, where the truth comes from, and how fresh it must be. Agreed by the owner on 2026-11-04 after a row-by-row review (55 rows; the row-by-row discussion is in the owner's review page and its backup, outside this repository). This file is the version to build from. A change to a row needs a decision line under "Changes" at the end.

## Principles

1. **Nothing rests on memory.** A rule is done only when a program enforces it or evidence shows it. A rule that lives in a note, a skill or a chat line is documentation, not a rule. The test: would it still hold after every session is cleared?
2. **One writer per fact.** Status is written by the probe (a script) from evidence; words (cards, asks, answers, queue order) are written by people and agents; nothing is typed that evidence can show.
3. **Three freshness classes.** *Live*: read by a script from git, GitHub, the deploy or the app, at most 1 minute old. *Instant*: a person or agent wrote it; shown within seconds. *Daily*: one row a day, by script.
4. **One store.** A Cloudflare Worker with D1 holds everything; the page, the probe, scripts and agents' tools read and write the same store. No model copies data (the Collector is deleted).
5. **Refuse early.** The Worker validates every board write against the rules in the same call. No hook judges a board write, or the wording of a message, after the fact. A hook may read facts (git, the board's wake file); it says nothing when they are in order.
6. **The board never stops work, and never does the work.** It cannot stop a commit, a review or a landing; it records what happened out of order and demands the explanation. It does not build, review or decide. What it does: it knows from evidence whose turn it is, and says so.
7. **Work moves by itself.** Models do judgment: building, reviewing, the verdict. Programs do hand-offs: the push, the wake of the next agent, and, where the owner has agreed, the landing and the deploy. No step waits on a chat message, on the lead's memory or on the owner. The only way to say "wait" is a hold with an end. The lead hears only about what does not move, with the evidence in the line.
8. **The owner's part is named and counted.** Everything the owner must do is an ask on the board: one thing, exact steps, what the owner should see after. Never a line to type in an agent's chat. A standing permission is installed once, where a program reads it. The board counts what waits on the owner and for how long.

## Where things live (after the refactor)

- **The store**: Cloudflare Worker + D1. Evidence (from the probe), cards, asks, threads, decisions, queue order, acks, the refusal log, usage rows.
- **Your browser**: values computed from the store at draw time (counts, ages, ETAs).
- **Truth comes from**: the live site (its `/version.json`), GitHub (branches, signed notes, main, CI runs, pull requests), the Claude app on the Studio (session list, transcripts), files the scripts leave on the Studio, people (what an agent typed or the owner clicked), and the board's own history.

## Freshness and wiring

- The probe runs on the Studio every 60 s (launchd), reads the truth sources and writes evidence to the store.
- The page fetches one JSON view of the store every 2–3 s while visible, with a version tag; unchanged answers cost bytes. Thread history loads on open.
- The probe asks the store every 20 s "anything for Manager?" and starts one Manager turn per batch. No Monitor, no re-arming, zero cost while quiet.
- Usage: the probe runs the usage script every 5 min (no model).

## Decisions taken in the review (2026-11-04)

- **Ask types** (Worker-enforced): Decision (question, 2–4 options, one recommended, screenshots for look questions), Action (numbered steps, copy boxes, "what you should see"), Terminal (commands on the Studio), Session start (exact words to type), Later (Manager's to-do for the owner; an ETA is required; the probe moves it into Needs you at the ETA or when its precondition is met), Approval (merge/release, data from GitHub, Approve/Withdraw read by the ship script), Answer (carries the question; Ack), Thread (free text, no buttons). Every ask has a title, who asked, since, and a card or "no card".
- **Card states** = phase + condition: Planning · Queued for <phase> behind #C (any phase) · Building · In review at <phase> · Rework · Signed · Shipping · Deploying · Live; overlays Gated, Blocked, Stalled, Paused, Dropped. Typed only: queue order, Blocked reasons, Paused, Dropped, the go on a plan. A typed status that contradicts evidence is refused.
- **Rework**: a commit on the tip after a sign-off drops the card to Rework automatically; Manager must attach a reason from a fixed list (bug found in test, review findings, design change, security finding, owner change); until then "Rework: reason missing" and other writes on the card are refused. Reasons stay in the card's history.
- **Tracks**: one phase list per repository in config. the product repository: Plan, Build, Test, Linguist, Design, Checker, Security, Ship, Live (review level None/Quick/Full and the customer-text flag decide skips). Flight board: Plan, Build, Test, Checker, Security, Pack, Install. Business data: Review, Approve, Merge, Live.
- **Tile states**: done (evidence), now (agent working or CI), queued (phase's agent busy), up next, skipped (level), stuck (over twice the median), gated (open ask). Hover shows the evidence.
- **Queues**: one column per agent: current card, then its queue as #C ids. the owner may reorder anything not started; his order outranks Manager's and is labelled "order set by the owner".
- **Versions**: estimate until signed (queue order, medians by size, stack switch; recomputed on reorder), firm once signed (main + 1 in READY order; stacked cards share a version). Tested by replaying the last 30 ships; estimate error reported.
- **Started** = first push of the branch to GitHub. **Stalled** = in any phase (not only Building): a card that has waited 15 min at its phase while that phase's agent is idle, with no CI or ship run going and no agent on the card; Gated, Blocked, Paused, Dropped and held (waiting on someone) cards are exempt. It is what wakes Manager (once per reason); the page's pill still says "Stalled" for an active card with no step logged for 30 min.
- **Live now**: the probe reads the site's `/version.json` every minute (XS product-repository card: the build writes it). "Deploying" from GitHub's running deploy.
- **Board version**: one number; the page reads its own tag, the probe reads main; mismatch shows "newer deployed, reload" or "deploy failed".
- **Refused**: the Worker logs refused writes (rule, card, time); the page shows them; cleared by the next accepted write.
- **Context tiles**: the probe is the sole writer (used / window per session); colours by pct; absolute numbers on hover; "window unknown" when unreadable; reset flow shown (due, resetting, fresh); alive/remote automatic; ↻ stays as "check now"; model · effort · window on the tile (effort readability to verify).
- **Messages**: the tick means "delivered HH:MM" (when the probe handed it to Manager). Unanswered items are counted by the Worker; nudge Manager at 10 min, push the owner at 30.
- **Ships and acks**: a feed of versions from main's commits, each listing its cards; the owner acks per ship; "went live, not acknowledged" is pushed to his phone.
- **Safe to restart**: every minute: no session outside the team active in 5 min, no agent active in 2 min, no scheduled run, no GitHub run, no ship lock, no pack install, no Remote Control. The tile names the first failing check. **Restart = drain then go**: a restart request sets "draining"; the probe tells every agent to finish its step and pause, Manager stops dispatching, Release finishes or skips its ship, scheduled tasks skip; the restart fires when the checks pass. Fallback: force at HH:MM, never cutting a ship.
- **Deleted**: the Collector, the mirror files, the write-gate and red-ask hooks, the Monitor/wake watch, the step log and typed phase/status, the "/clear me" flag, the Refresh request, the keeper line, the "data behind" line, the v3 layout and plan outline, the stuck-agent watchdog task (folds into the probe).
