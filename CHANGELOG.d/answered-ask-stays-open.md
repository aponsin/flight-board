The lead kept two rules in memory: never close an ask the owner just answered or marked Done, he closes it; never resolve his thread with an agent's post. The second already holds in the store (#C1046: an agent's post cannot carry `resolved`, a test pins it again here). The first is now a refusal: `closeRefusal` (`worker/board-rules.mjs`) refuses an agent's `ask_close` with the rule `close-owner-just-answered` for an hour after the owner's answer, even when his word is the newest on the thread, saying he closes it; an hour on, the #C1046 rule decides as before. The owner's own close is never held, an ask he has not answered is as before, and an Approval (the Reviewer's housekeeping when its pull request merges) is exempt. Ships: a Worker deploy.
