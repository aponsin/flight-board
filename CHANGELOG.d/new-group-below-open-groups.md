- A new group starts below the open groups of its repository (new-group-below-open-groups, #C1372). On 11-20 the owner's screenshot showed board-polish-fourteen, made after board-polish-thirteen, above it in the group order (2 of 5 against 3 of 5). The cause: a group nobody had placed sorted after the placed ones by its batch id, and "board-polish-fourteen" is before "board-polish-thirteen" by letters, so a new group could land above an older open one. Now a group nobody placed sorts by its #G number (minted when the group is made, so the newest is last) and only then by id, in the one rule the store, the dispatch, the stall line, the first-push check and the page all read (`groupRank`, with `orderListOf` building the list from the placed rows and the group numbers). Nothing is written to the order: no row exists until the owner places a group, so the owner's order is still written only by the owner (the DATA.md row for the group order now says that new groups go by number, with a dated DATA.d line); a placed group still goes by its place, the owner's moves stick, a group with no number known ranks after the numbered ones by id as before, and groups of other repositories rank by when they were made. New `tests/new-group-goes-last.test.mjs` (the store, the pure rule, nothing written, the owner's move sticking), a page test in `tests/owner-orders-groups.test.mjs` and a first-push test in `tests/dispatch-enforces-group-order.test.mjs`; mutation-checked: ignoring the number in the rule fails the store, rule, page and first-push tests, ignoring it on the page fails the page test, and dropping the group numbers from the queue file fails the first-push test.
