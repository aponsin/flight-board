- A thread the owner closed with an "Ack." stays closed until someone writes in it (closed-thread-resurfaces). On 11-17 a thread whose last word was his Ack, stored resolved, with nobody writing after it, came back under "with the team" and he asked why. The page held an Ack open whenever any card naming the thread was in flight, even a card made after his word (a follow-up card that cites the thread). His Ack now leaves the thread with the team only while a card that was already in flight when he wrote it still is; a card made later does not reopen it, and a new message in the thread still does.
