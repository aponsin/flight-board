- A stall that has lasted 30 minutes also wakes the stalled agent (stalls-wake-agent-directly, #C1097). On 11-16, from about 5 am to 10 am MT, three signed cards sat with Boardsmith and #C1095 with Release: the stalls reached Manager's inbox and nothing woke the agents. An idle-agent stall (a card waiting at a phase for an idle role, or a queued card for an idle owner) that the store first raised 30 minutes ago now also gives that role a DISPATCH line, `DISPATCH #C<n> at stall: it has waited for you 30 minutes while you were idle; read it on the board and pick it up now`, by the dispatch's own path; the stall still goes to Manager's inbox as before. The line prints once per stall: a changed reason (the agent worked and went idle again, the card moved on) is a new line, and the lead gets none. It reaches only an open session whose dispatch watch is running; waking a closed session stays with the app. Tests: `tests/stall-wakes-agent.test.mjs`.
