- A card writes its docs/DATA.md "Changes" line to its own file, and a command folds the files in (data-md-change-fragments, #C1090). Every card appended its line at the end of DATA.md, so each landing conflicted every waiting branch there and cost a rebase and a re-sign (five rebases on 11-16). Now `docs/DATA.d/<card id>.md` holds the line, and `node scripts/data-fragments.mjs --write` folds the files in, oldest date first; `--check` tests them. Existing lines stay as they are. Tests: `tests/data-fragments.test.mjs`.
