- A card writes its docs/DATA.md "Changes" line to its own file, and one fold card joins the files in (data-md-change-fragments, #C1090). Every card appended its line at the end of DATA.md, so each landing conflicted every waiting branch there and cost a rebase and a re-sign (five rebases on 11-16). Now `docs/DATA.d/<card id>.md` holds the line, and the fold card (a Board-Id starting `fold-data-fragments`) runs `node scripts/data-fragments.mjs --write`, which folds the files in, oldest date first; `--check` tests them. The landing job refuses any other tip that adds a dated line to docs/DATA.md (a row or a principle may still be edited), and reports FOLD DUE when 5 or more fragments wait on main, which wakes Boardsmith (once a day) to cut the fold card. Existing lines stay as they are. Ships: the landing job, the dispatch and the dispatch line texts are pinned, so they ride the next owner pack. Tests: `tests/data-fragments.test.mjs`, `tests/landing-refuses-data-md-edit.test.mjs`, `tests/fold-due-wakes-boardsmith.test.mjs`.
