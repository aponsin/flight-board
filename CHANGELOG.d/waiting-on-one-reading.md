- A card's "waiting on" now means the same thing to the dispatch, the stall checks and the page: if it names someone other than the card's owner, the card is held on them for everyone (no stall line, no wake for the owner, a plain row and never "stalled" on the page); if it names the owner itself, or is empty, it holds nothing. Before, "next is Checker" could keep the stall checks quiet while the dispatch kept waking the owner and the page showed the card stalled (waiting-on-one-reading, #C1164). Ships: a Worker deploy and a page publish, plus script changes that ride the next owner pack. Tests: `tests/waiting-on-one-reading.test.mjs`.
