- The watchdog task's restart and prompt-ledger steps become probe readings, with no script of another repository (watchdog-into-probe #C1005, part c). The probe keeps its own marker (`watch-state.json`: the roster's session ids and the ledger's line count) and posts a `watch` reading only on a run with something to say: the app restarted (every session it knew is gone while some run), the permission prompts and other notifications the prompt ledger (`collector.promptLedger`, an absolute `.jsonl` path in board.config.json, read only, at most 8 MB, empty for none) gained since the last run, counted by role and tool (never a command or a message), and "Prompt ledger silent" when the ledger has had no line for 24 h while agents worked, told once per newest line. The store turns each into one stall row in Manager's batch, told once (`watch:restart|ledger|quiet:<time>`), from a reading under 15 min old. Simplest readings, noted on the card: the first run takes the ledger's length without reporting its history; "running" for the silence is the agents the probe reads as working; the watchdog's "its own runs were missed" restart rule has no meaning for a probe that runs every minute and is left out; the marker is saved when the run's local files are written, so a failed post is not retried. The schema gains `watch` (`collector/state-schema.mjs`); a `watch` that does not fit is dropped, never the whole state. Tests: `tests/watch-readings.test.mjs`. Not in this commit: the one-day side-by-side proof and pausing the watchdog task, which follow the landing and the owner's pack.
