- A card may say what it removes (card-says-what-it-removes, #C866; the owner's "lean, delete" of 2026-11-04, the audit's "every board card says what it removes"). `card_create` and `card_update` take an optional `removes`, one plain line of up to 300 characters (no line break or control character; null clears it); the store keeps it in a new nullable column (migration 0034), the view carries it to the page's feed, and the card's Details fold draws "Removes: <the line>" beside the Next line, escaped. A card written without it is as before. Ships: a Worker deploy with migration 0034 and the page. Tests: `tests/card-removes.test.mjs` (mutation-checked: the store column, the length limit, the control-character check, the card-word list, the view and the page's escape each fail a test).
