- A thread folds and grays out the moment its answer is sent (board-thread-sending-state, #C1233; the owner, mockup A: after a click he could not tell whether it went). Any answer button, Ack, Close, a decision option or Send reply, folds the thread to its title bar at once, turns it gray (`details.sending`: `grayscale`, the muted text colour and a tint of the board's own line and panel colours, so it follows both themes) and shows three small pulsing dots under its id (`.sdots`, still when motion is reduced), in a Needs you or Waiting on Manager row, a thread row and a card's thread alike (`markSending`, `applySending`, `sendingTaken` in `page/board.template.html`). It stays usable: a click opens it (the fold is remembered in `folds`, so a redraw keeps it as he left it) and a follow-up sends as any message does and starts the state again. The state ends when Manager has taken the message (the watch's delivered time reaches it, only while the watch is live) or the thread has a newer word from Manager; after 10 minutes it ends anyway, and a write that fails clears it at once. A redraw of a row puts the class and the dots back (`applySending` runs after a whole redraw and after a part's). The new-thread form has no row and is left as it was. Ships: a page change, so a pack with a hooks step. Tests: `tests/board-thread-sending-state.test.mjs`.
