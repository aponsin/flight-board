- An owner's closing line no longer turns a thread red "unanswered" (owner-closer-not-unanswered #C1039). On 11-12 #T1880, #T1887, #T1892 and #T1893 showed "unanswered" and "Manager answering" though Manager had answered each: the owner's newest word was a closer ("ok", "I'll let you know", "archived it", "Chose B: ..."), and the page counted any newest owner word as a new question. Now, after the team has spoken, his word leaves the thread answered when it asks nothing: no "?", no files, and either a decision pick (the page's own "Chose K: label") or a short closer (60 characters, one line: an acknowledgement, "I'll let you know", "archived it", "thanks", with no request after it). A follow-up still turns it red: a "?", a request without one ("Please add ..."), a closer with a request in it ("thanks, but fix it"), a long note, a file. A lone owner "ok" with no team word before it still waits, as before. Page only (`threadOpen` in `page/board.template.html`); it ships with the next page deploy.
