- An owner's closing line no longer turns a thread red "unanswered" (owner-closer-not-unanswered #C1039). On 11-12 #T1880, #T1887, #T1892 and #T1893 showed "unanswered" and "Manager answering" though Manager had answered each: the owner's newest word was a closer ("ok", "I'll let you know", "archived it", "Chose B: ..."), and the page counted any newest owner word as a new question. Now, after the team has spoken, his word leaves the thread answered only when the WHOLE message is a known closer (Manager's ruling after Checker's findings, since every rule built from words or sentence forms let a request through: "check it again", "you deleted it"). After trimming, lowercasing and dropping end punctuation it equals one of: ok, okay, ack, closed, done, thanks, thank you, archived it, got it, I'll let you know, I will let you know; or it is exactly one of the options Manager's decision offered ("Chose B: <its label>", as the page sends it, nothing after it); or it is his two-sentence "I'll try <something>. I'll let you know" with nothing in it that pushes back or asks (the owner's "I'll try the reset when I am closer to 100% usage. I'll let you know"). No "?", no files, one line. Everything else is a follow-up and stays red, rare closers included ("ok thanks", "sounds good", "deleted it"): a red ping on a closer is cheap, a missed question is not. A lone owner "ok" with no team word before it still waits, as before. Page only (`threadOpen` in `page/board.template.html`); it ships with the next page deploy.
