On 11-12 #T1880, #T1887, #T1892 and #T1893 showed "unanswered" and "Manager answering" though Manager had answered each: the owner's newest word was a closer ("ok", "I'll let you know", "archived it", "Chose B: ..."), and the page counted any newest owner word as a new question. Now, after the team has spoken, his word leaves the thread answered only on an allow list (Checker's finding: a list of words to refuse let most requests through): no "?", no files, one line, and either every word is a closer word or a harmless filler ("ok thanks", "got it, thanks", "archived it", "I'll let you know", "will do"), or the text is exactly one of the options Manager's decision offered ("Chose B: <its label>", as the page sends it), or it starts with "I" and ends on "I'll let you know" with nothing in it that pushes back or asks (the owner's "I'll try the reset when I am closer to 100% usage. I'll let you know"). Anything else is a follow-up and stays red: "thanks, revert it", "ok, but ...", "Please add ...", a pick with anything after it, a pick Manager did not offer. A lone owner "ok" with no team word before it still waits, as before. Page only (`threadOpen` in `page/board.template.html`); it ships with the next page deploy.
