- The watch no longer tells a builder to re-cut a lower card of a stack whose tip has main (behind-main-reads-stack-tip). On 11-18 it sent "signed but behind main, re-cut on main" for two lower cards of a stack whose fully signed tip already had main merged in; a re-cut would have cost every signature. A card read under a stack tip is now behind main only through the stack's tip: the lower cards carry no re-cut of their own, and the tip card is re-cut, once, when the tip itself lacks main. A lone card signed and behind main is re-cut as before. Ships: the probe, with the next owner pack. Tests: `tests/behind-main-reads-stack-tip.test.mjs` (real git).
