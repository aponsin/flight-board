- An answered plan or decision cannot be closed by an agent until its steps name cards (plan-steps-become-cards, #C863, half 1 of 3). The Worker refuses `ask_close` from an agent on an answered Decision (or any answered ask whose answer says it "becomes a card") while the answer lists steps (numbered, dashed, starred or bulleted lines, or "Step 1:") that carry no card number (#C123): the refusal is `plan-steps-uncarded` and lists the uncarded steps. The ask's own question and option text count too (a plan posted as a Decision whose question lists the steps, answered "A: go"), so a question that uses "- " bullets for context now blocks an agent's close until each such line names a card; the owner can always close. "1.5 hours", "-1 point" and a bare "-" are not steps. Every step carded, or no steps listed, closes as before; the owner's own close is never refused. The board does not make a card per step: the agent makes them and names them. Worker deploy only, no migration. Halves 2 and 3 are plan-part-on-main and plan-file-without-card. Tests: `tests/plan-steps-uncarded.test.mjs`.
