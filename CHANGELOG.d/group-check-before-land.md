- A group head is checked as a whole before it lands (#C1146, split out of #C1068). Each card is reviewed alone, but not how the cards meet, so the landing job now wants, on a head that carries two or more cards, Checker's READ note naming every file that two or more of the cards touch (`node scripts/group-check.mjs read --sha <head> --signer "Checker 2" --push`; `files` lists them), and one run of the full suite on the head, made by the job itself and kept per commit. Without the note the head waits and names the files; a red suite is a refusal Release sees. A head of one card is unchanged. `scripts/group-check.mjs`, `scripts/board-land.mjs`, `LAND_SUITE_SEC` in `worker/probe-jobs.mjs`, `docs/group-land.md`. Ships: the owner's pack, with its land step. Tests: `tests/group-check-before-land.test.mjs`.
