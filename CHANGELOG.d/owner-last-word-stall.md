- A thread that ends on the owner's word with no reply now raises a stall line for Manager until someone posts in it (#C1081; on 11-16 three threads were left unanswered because read_inbox shows a message once). A thread whose newest message is the owner's, 10 minutes old or more, from the last 7 days, and still open (its ask not closed or withdrawn, its card not dropped or done, the message not marked resolved) gets one line, and again with a reminder number, until anyone posts in it: every 30 minutes for the first four reminders, then every 3 hours. A word that only acknowledges ("Ack.", "Closed.", "Done", "ok", "thanks"; the page's own ack list, plus closed and done) raises none, and the newest message decides. The line holds the thread's number and the owner's time, never the message text. Change in `worker/board-store.mjs` and `worker/push-rules.mjs`; no migration. Ships: a Worker deploy. Tests: `tests/owner-last-word-stall.test.mjs`.
