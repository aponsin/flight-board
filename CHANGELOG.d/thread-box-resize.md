- The thread's scroll box can be dragged taller by its bottom corner, and a phone's box grows to fit (thread-box-resize, #C1292; the owner wanted a taller box). `page/board.template.html`: the box (`.t-hist`) is `resize: vertical` with a visible handle (the browser's own, drawn in the muted ink in Chrome and Safari). It is content-high up to 19rem as before until the first press on its bottom-right corner; the script then fixes its height and adds `.sized`, which gives it a floor of 6rem and lifts the cap to 80% of the screen height. A real drag (a move of 2 px or more) is remembered in this browser only (localStorage, as the read marks are), as one height for every thread box, and applied after each redraw as the smaller of it and what the thread needs, so a short thread is not padded. A browser with no usable storage forgets it and still works. On a phone (600 px and narrower) the box has no inner scroll and no handle: it grows to fit its messages and the page scrolls past it, replacing #C1232's half-screen cap there. Unchanged: the laptop's default height, the scroll position kept across a redraw, the soft shadow that says there is more below. The page changes, so it rides the next board pack. Tests: `tests/thread-box-resize.test.mjs` (the CSS, the corner press, the saved drag, the redraw, no storage).
