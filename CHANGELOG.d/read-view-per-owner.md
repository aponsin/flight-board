- An agent can now read its own part of the board (read-view-per-owner, #C1089). The `read_view` tool returned the whole board, about 5.4 MB with 4816 messages, which no agent could read: Ops could not list its own open cards after its pickup. It now takes `owner_role` (that role's open cards and the open asks it asked or that sit on one of those cards), `thread_id` (one thread and its messages) and `full: true` (the whole view, for a check that needs every ask: `scripts/approvals-check.mjs` passes it). With no argument it returns a short summary (the live and probe state, open cards and asks counted per role). The page, the probe and the dispatch read the store's own view and are not changed. Ships: a Worker deploy; `scripts/approvals-check.mjs` rides whichever pack carries it. Tests: `tests/read-view-filters.test.mjs`.
