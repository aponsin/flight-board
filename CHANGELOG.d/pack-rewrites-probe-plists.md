- A pack that changes a probe job's time limit now carries the step that moves its LaunchAgent too (pack-rewrites-probe-plists). Since the ask job's limit went from 30 s to 65 s, its LaunchAgent still ended it at 45 s, so the second send of a retry was cut. `scripts/make-pair.mjs` compares the probe jobs' table (`worker/probe-jobs.mjs`) with the last pack's and, for the jobs that changed, writes `board-store-probes-pc<n>.mjs`: it rewrites each one's alarm (limit plus 15 s) and interval, with a backup, a lint, a reload and a read-back, and an undo. No change, no step. Ships: the pack tool; the step runs on the owner's Mac when he runs the pack line. Tests: `tests/pack-rewrites-probe-plists.test.mjs`.
