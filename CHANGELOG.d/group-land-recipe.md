- `docs/group-land.md` writes down how a group lands as one merged head: the repository's builder merges the signed card tips in stack order and folds the data fragments, Checker and Security each sign every commit of `origin/main..head` with a note of its own, Release lands that one head and the builder cuts one pack from main. It names the three traps of G17: a line note covers nothing over a merge or more than 30 commits, the release tool wants both roles' notes on every commit (a Quick card's too), and an early commit marked SUPERSEDED, SUPERSEDED-BY-REBASE or WIP blocks the land once a merge brings it into main's history (group-land-recipe, #C1130). Docs only: no install, no code.
