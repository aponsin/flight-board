- The dispatch stops waking an agent for a new card while it has a started group not yet READY (#C1080; the dispatch woke Boardsmith three times in the middle of G9, for #C997, #C990 and #C1078, and Manager held each by hand). A role has an open group when it owns a card in a started group (a group with a push, a landing or a card closed as done) that still has to be built: nothing pushed, or pushed with no stack-ready signal covering its head. While one is open, the role's new-build turns for cards outside that group are dropped; the group's own cards, reviews, rework, landing and every other turn are untouched. A card of the group that is paused, dropped, done, blocked, live, held by an open hold, waiting on an open ask or waiting on someone else does not keep it open, so a stuck card cannot silence an agent for ever. A group card held by a depends_on keeps its group open only when its blockers are the same role's cards, and those blockers (and theirs) are still built, in or out of the group, so the group cannot wait on a card the dispatch withholds. With no group open the queue is dispatched as before. Change in `collector/store-dispatch.mjs` (a pinned file: it rides the owner's install). No Worker, store or page change. Tests: `tests/dispatch-respects-open-group.test.mjs`.
