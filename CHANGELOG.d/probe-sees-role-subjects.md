- The probe finds a card's head from a signed commit written "#C<num> (<Role>): ..." and from a branch whose last segment starts "c<num>-" (probe-sees-role-subjects). On 11-17 a card built with a role in its commit subject, no Board-Id trailer and a branch named only by number had no head on the board, so the next card's stack-ready said its group waited on a card that "was not built". The head is now found by either, in both readers (the probe's own copy and the board's), and a numbered branch alone, on main, shows the card as being worked, never as shipped. A subject without a colon or bracket, a number that only starts with the same digits, and a number in the middle of a line still match nothing. A head found only by its number whose tip is another card's commit (a branch cut on a signed, unshipped tip) is read as work started (build) with none of that tip's sign-offs, and the weekly sweep of shipped branches keeps a numbered branch of an open card. Tests: `tests/probe-sees-role-subjects.test.mjs`, `tests/probe-numbered-branch-stacked.test.mjs`.
