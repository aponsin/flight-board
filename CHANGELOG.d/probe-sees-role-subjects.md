- The probe finds a card's head from a signed commit written "#C<num> (<Role>): ..." and from a branch whose last segment starts "c<num>-" (probe-sees-role-subjects). On 11-17 a card built with a role in its commit subject, no Board-Id trailer and a branch named only by number had no head on the board, so the next card's stack-ready said its group waited on a card that "was not built". The head is now found by either, in both readers (the probe's own copy and the board's), and a numbered branch alone, on main, shows the card as being worked, never as shipped. A subject without a colon or bracket, a number that only starts with the same digits, and a number in the middle of a line still match nothing. Tests: `tests/probe-sees-role-subjects.test.mjs`.
