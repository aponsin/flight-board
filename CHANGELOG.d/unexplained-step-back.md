- A step back needs a reason (unexplained-step-back, #C1114 part 2). A card read at a review phase and then back at plan, build or test with no FINDINGS note and no reason no longer moves: the board keeps its review phase and marks it "unexplained rework" until a reason is attached (a WIP push before READY is never one). The reasons are one list, now with "wording" (bug found in test, review findings, design change, security finding, owner change, wording), and each accepted reason adds 1 to its counter once, on the write that attaches it; "review findings" adds none, since the notes already count those. Ships as a Worker deploy with migration 0036. The page's red chip and the reviewer's wake follow in their own commits. Tests: `tests/unexplained-step-back.test.mjs`.
