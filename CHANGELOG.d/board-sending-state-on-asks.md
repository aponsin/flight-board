- The sending state is on everything the owner's click or text sends to Manager, through one path (board-sending-state-on-asks, #C1241; the owner, on #T2091 after Done: the fold, gray and dots of #C1233 and #C1235 were on threads only, and he asked for the same everywhere, for consistency). Asks in Needs you and Later (`onReply`: Done, "Something went wrong, help", a note's Send, a decision's option, OK, "Change something", "Something else", Ack, Reply) now fold to their title line, gray out and show the dots in place of their type label at the click, exactly as a thread does (the ask's id is the key `markSending` already used for threads); the ask stays clickable to reopen and add a note, and the state ends when Manager has taken it. A write that fails, or a file that cannot be stored, opens the ask again with its "Not saved" line (`clearSending(id, true)`). The new-thread form is sent through the same path too: the thread's row, drawn by the write, carries the state. The controls that are not a row to fold take the dots in place of their own label (`.sbtn`, the label's text kept for width and a reader): a thread's Ping, ↻ Check on a card, Ideas "Let's start", and a context tile's ↻ refresh. A button's key is `b:<kind>:<id>` (`markButton`, `btnKeyOf`, `btnThread`); it ends when the delivered time reaches it, when Manager writes in the thread the button writes to, or after 10 minutes, and a failed write ends it at once; `pressCheck` now says whether its write failed (it swallowed the error before), so ↻ Check can end its state. A redraw puts every state back (`applySending`). Not covered, by the owner's word: the agent PRs' Approve and Withdraw (they go to Release), thread Rename, and the To-do and Ideas Done and arrows (they send nothing to Manager). Same look and colours as threads, light and dark, phone too. Ships: a page change, so a pack with a hooks step. Tests: `tests/board-thread-sending-state.test.mjs`.
