- A new card needs a group (card-create-needs-group, #C1107). On 11-16 15 of 27 cards were loose because grouping lived in the lead's memory. The `card_create` tool now refuses a card with no `batch` (rule `card-group`), and the refusal lists the open groups, newest first, up to 12 and a count of the rest; the `card_update` tool refuses a write that clears `batch` (null or blank), so a card changes group only by moving to another one or by the owner's pull-out. A write to a card that has no group yet is refused unless it sets `batch` (the lead's ruling, Checker 2's findings on 401d8139); a write that closes the card (`done` with its evidence) or drops it needs none, and a card already done or dropped is not asked for one. The summary rule of #C1087 stands beside it and is checked first. Ships: a Worker deploy. Tests: `tests/card-create-needs-group.test.mjs` (the tool wiring included); the older tests that made cards through the tool now give them a group.
