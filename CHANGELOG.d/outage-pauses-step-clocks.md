- A stretch when the probe is silent no longer counts against a step (outage-pauses-step-clocks, #C990). On 11-11 the disk filled, the probe could not run for hours, and #C964's Checker step turned red as if Checker had stalled. Now the store records each silence of 5 minutes or more as an outage, and the stall and slow lines take the outage time out of how long a step has waited: a step already late before the outage stays late, one that was not does not turn late because of it, and a real stall outside an outage still fires. The header's Probe chip says "silent since <time>, step clocks paused". Not covered yet: an outage the probe cannot see (the probe up, a role's machine down) and the page's own red clocks. Ships as a Worker deploy (migration 0035). Tests: `tests/outage-pauses-step-clocks.test.mjs`.
