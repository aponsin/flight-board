- The owner orders the card groups, and the order steers the work (owner-orders-groups #C1053; the owner chose A on 11-12): the board lists the groups top to bottom in the order they go live, and each group row has real up and down buttons (keyboard reachable, 44 px on a phone) that move it one place. The store keeps the order in a new `group_order` table (Worker migration 0033; written only by the owner from the page, `/api/group-move`, or by Manager's typed `group_move` as the fallback; every other writer is refused and logged as `group-move-owner`). A new group goes to the bottom, ahead of nothing the owner has placed; a card in no group is listed and dispatched after every group; a group with no open card drops out of the order at the next move. The dispatch gives each agent its next card from the highest group that has one for its role, then by queue_pos inside the group, so an agent with nothing in the top group gets the next group's card. Groups are worked whole (the owner: "we work on groups and don't segment them"): once a group has started (one of its cards pushed, landed or closed as done), an agent finishes every card it owns there before it takes a card from another group; the owner's order decides which group starts next. Ships: a Worker deploy (the migration and the route), the pinned `collector/store-dispatch.mjs` (the owner's install) and a page publish. Designer looks at the buttons. Tests: `tests/owner-orders-groups.test.mjs`, `tests/card-groups-page.test.mjs`.
