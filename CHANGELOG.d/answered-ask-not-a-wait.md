- An ask the owner has answered no longer makes its card wait on the owner (answered-ask-not-a-wait, #C1398): the reading's `ask` wait is an open ask that is neither answered nor withdrawn (a withdrawn ask is no wait either), so a card whose ask was answered but not yet closed no longer reads a red "ASKED #n" tile and "WAITING ON YOU", and falls through to its next wait or state; the ask itself stays open until it is closed, and the ask lists, the owner's inbox, the open-decision gates, the dispatch and the stall lines still read it as open. Worker only (`worker/compat-reading.mjs`); no pack.
