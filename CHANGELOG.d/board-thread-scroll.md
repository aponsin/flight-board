- A long thread scrolls inside its own box (board-thread-scroll, #C1232; the owner: an open thread with many back-and-forths took too much height). The messages of a thread sit in `.t-hist`, at most 19 rem tall (about four messages; on a phone at most 48% of the screen, so the page can still be scrolled past it) with its own scroll bar; the reply box and its buttons stay above it, outside the scroll, and the order is as it was (newest message first, so it opens at the newest). A redraw keeps the box's place (`histSave` in `page/board.template.html`, used by `keep()` and `anchored()`): a reader at the newest end stays there and sees a new message; one who scrolled down to older messages keeps the same messages in view, the height added above him moves his scroll. A thread with no message draws no box. A soft shadow at the bottom of the box says there is more below while it is (Designer's F1: Mac and iPhone hide scroll bars until a scroll): a panel-coloured cover that scrolls with the content over a fixed shadow, CSS only; its colour is the token `--hist-shade` (dark on the light panel, light on the dark one, Designer's F2: a black shadow cannot show on a near-black panel). Ships: a page change, so a pack with a hooks step. Tests: `tests/board-thread-scroll.test.mjs`.
