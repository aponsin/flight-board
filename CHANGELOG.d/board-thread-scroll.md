- A long thread scrolls inside its own box (board-thread-scroll, #C1232; the owner: an open thread with many back-and-forths took too much height). The messages of a thread sit in `.t-hist`, at most 19 rem tall (about four messages; on a phone at most 48% of the screen, so the page can still be scrolled past it) with its own scroll bar; the reply box and its buttons stay above it, outside the scroll, and the order is as it was (newest message first, so it opens at the newest). A redraw keeps the box's place (`histSave` in `page/board.template.html`, used by `keep()` and `anchored()`): a reader at the newest end stays there and sees a new message; one who scrolled down to older messages keeps the same messages in view, the height added above him moves his scroll. A thread with no message draws no box. Ships: a page change, so a pack with a hooks step. Tests: `tests/board-thread-scroll.test.mjs`.
