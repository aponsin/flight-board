- A card's bug counters follow its range (bug-count-follows-range, #C1397). The counters only ever rose, so a card whose range changed (a re-cut onto another base, or the stack under it landing on main) kept findings that belonged to commits no longer between main and its tip: #C1386 and #C1388 showed 1 bug while the probe's own count for their range was 0. The probe now sends `issueBase` (the merge-base of main and the tip); when it differs from the one in the card's previous reading the Worker sets the bugs, security, language and design counters to the new range's counts (they may go down, an empty range clears them), `owner` and `release` untouched; within one range they still only rise, and a card with no earlier `issueBase` keeps today's raise-only rule for one reading. `collector/state.mjs` and `collector/state-schema.mjs` are PINNED PROBE CODE (rides the next owner pack); the Worker change ships as a Worker deploy. Test: `tests/bug-count-follows-range.test.mjs`.
