- A group reads "building" only when one of its cards is. A group none of whose cards is started says "queued" and lights no tile; a built card that waits for its group reads "Built, waiting" (the probe marks a held card whose exact head carries the stack-ready waiting note, `builtWaiting`), and a group with only such cards does not pulse. A commit on a `build/` branch is pushed to origin at once by a new post-commit hook (`.githooks/post-commit`, never forced, no reviewer is woken because a pushed tip waits for READY, #C1066), so the board shows the work within a probe run (group-building-label-truth, #C1102). Ships: a probe and page change, and the hook with the next checkout. Tests: `tests/group-building-label-truth.test.mjs`, `tests/post-commit-push.test.mjs`, `tests/group-pulses-only-when-worked.test.mjs`.
