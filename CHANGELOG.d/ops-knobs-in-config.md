- The probe's cadence numbers are settings you can change in the board config without a reinstall (ops-knobs-in-config, #C997 part A). A new `ops` section takes seconds for the evidence job's interval and limit, the slow-run mark, and the page's two five-minute edges (an evidence reading is stale, the probe is silent); it is read at every run. A value that is not a number, too low or too high is ignored, the default stands, and the page header says which setting was ignored and why. Ships as a Worker deploy and the owner's pack. Tests: `tests/knobs.test.mjs`, `tests/ops-knobs-wiring.test.mjs`.
