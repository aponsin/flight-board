- A card is not read as live while a part of it is not on main (plan-part-on-main, #C890, half 2 of #C863). A part is named by the word "part" and a commit sha in the card's next_note ("part 1a2b3c4", 7 to 40 hex characters); every other sha in a note (a superseded tip, a review tip, an old stack) is ignored. While any marked sha is not an ancestor of main, the probe holds the card at ship instead of live and shows which sha is missing (`partsMissing` on its reading); a sha the repository cannot find counts as not on main. A note with no marker behaves as before. Probe only (a probe install, no Worker deploy). Tests: `tests/plan-part-on-main.test.mjs`.
