- A card is not read as live while a part of it is not on main (plan-part-on-main, #C890, half 2 of #C863). A part is named by a commit sha cited in the card's next_note (7 to 40 hex characters): while any cited sha is not an ancestor of main, the probe holds the card at ship instead of live and shows which sha is missing (`partsMissing` on its reading); a sha the repository cannot find counts as not on main. A note with no sha behaves as before. Probe only (a probe install, no Worker deploy). Tests: `tests/plan-part-on-main.test.mjs`.
