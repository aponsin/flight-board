- The probe reads every card on a group branch (probe-reads-group-branch #C1044): a branch's tip names only its last card, so the other members of a group had no head, read as not built, and stack-ready and the dispatch's batch hold waited forever. For a branch whose tip's card has a batch, the Board-Id trailer of every commit in `main..tip` now gives each open batched card with no head of its own that commit as its head. The group tip's notes cover every member under it (the same cover the stack path passes), so tip-only PASS and SECURE line notes read as signed for all members and a FINDINGS on the tip reaches them: one review per group, not one per member (Checker 2's finding on 63437c45). A card with no batch, a single-card branch and a card with a head of its own are unchanged.
