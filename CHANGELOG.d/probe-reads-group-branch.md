- The probe reads every card on a group branch (probe-reads-group-branch #C1044): a branch's tip names only its last card, so the other members of a group had no head, read as not built, and stack-ready and the dispatch's batch hold waited forever. For a branch whose tip's card has a batch, the Board-Id trailer of every commit in `main..tip` now gives each open batched card with no head of its own that commit as its head. A card with no batch, a single-card branch and a card with a head of its own are unchanged.
