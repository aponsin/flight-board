- A card shows the replies in its thread that you have not read yet (card-thread-unread-replies, #C1266). `page/board.template.html`: a "N new replies" pill on the card's title line, the Thread label in the accent, and each new reply on a soft accent background with a "new" tag; collapsing the thread clears them. Which replies you have read is kept in this browser only (localStorage, per device), so the phone and the laptop each keep their own count, and nothing is written to the store. Unchanged: the "Thread (N)" label and count, the badge, the tick under your own message, and every thread drawn outside a card. The page changes, so it rides the next board pack.
