- Card groups on the board (card-groups-on-board #C1008, the owner's look A of 11-12). Each group of cards that land together is one row: "GROUP", its number and name from its batch id ("g3-probe-and-watchdog" reads "G3 · Probe and watchdog"; any other id shows as it is), how many cards, one phase strip lit at its earliest card's phase (the group lands together, so it moves at its slowest card), the latest card's ETA and the earliest card's start. Its cards hang under it on a blue rail, one line each with the card's own state (Queued, Building, Built, Blocked, Paused) and its non-zero issue chips; a click opens the card's full row. On the store page each has a Pull out button that asks first ("Pull #C977 out? Yes" for 4 s; only a tap on that posts): the card leaves its group (the owner only, `POST /api/pull-out`), and its row is tagged "↰ pulled out of G3 · 10:05 AM by <owner>" until it is put in a group again.
