- A review's ETA counts the reviewer's mutants, and a late review wakes Manager (review-eta-counts-mutants, #C1115 part 2). The claim_review tool takes `mutants` (how many the reviewer will run), `mutant_sec` (the seconds one takes: the mutant runner prints its median; 150 s each when none is given) and `progress` (one line); the page's review ETA adds count x seconds to the checker median, and a review 30 minutes past that ETA sends Manager one line with the reviewer's progress line (a new progress line is a new wake; releasing or signing the claim ends the spell). Before, 30 mutants took about 75 minutes the ETA never counted, and G6 missed its time by more than an hour. Ships as a Worker deploy (migration 0037, four optional columns on `review_claims`) and a page publish. Tests: `tests/review-eta-counts-mutants.test.mjs`.
