- The dispatch skips a card whose owner has an open decision to answer on a thread (#C1073; 11-12: Builder's watch fired "DISPATCH #C1071 ... build it" on every re-arm while the owner had an A or B to answer). A Decision the lead posts in a thread (kind `decision`, no ask row, #C920) is open until the owner has written in that thread after it, or a later message of the thread is marked resolved; the card it names (a `Card: <id>` line, else the one `#C<n>` in its first line) is held like a card with an open ask: no build, review or other turn, until it is answered, and then it is dispatched again. `collector/store-dispatch.mjs` (`openDecisionCards`) holds it for the dispatch; the Worker's idle-agent line reads the same rule (`worker/board-rules.mjs`, a copy a test holds equal), so it never counts or names such a card as queued. Ships: the owner's pack (the dispatch is pinned) and a Worker deploy. Tests: `tests/dispatch-skips-open-decision.test.mjs`.
