- Step 1 of the one reading per card: the module that says where a card is (one-reading-module, #C1380, the owner's pick A on #T2194, Director's audit #C1375 and its contract). `worker/reading.mjs` has one pure function, `reading(card, evidence, claims, agents, config, now, ctx)`, that returns `phase`, `state` (done, unknown, rework, working, waiting, stuck or queued, the first that holds), `wait` (turn, group, land, pack, install, deploy, ask or hold), `with` (one role: the owner at plan, build and rework; the claim holder or the repository's reviewer at a review phase; the release role at ship; the owner of the board at install and ask), `working`, `since`, `asOf` and `why`, from rules that already exist and are pure (`isRework`, `holdOpen`, `groupWaits`, `reviewerOf`, the lane roles, `repoKind`). Closed means live: a card closed at ship reads as waiting for its deploy, never done, and a product repository never has a pack or an install. A repository word the config's list does not know reads unknown, never board. A stale reading keeps its last good state and carries `stale` and `staleSince`, is never working and never done; with no earlier good reading it is unknown, and never both. This step adds the module, its definition (`docs/DATA.d/one-reading-module.md`, Director's section A), eleven real readings (`tests/fixtures/readings-real.json`: evidence taken from the live board, the repository names made neutral) and the laws that hold today (done implies live, a product card has no pack or install, never stale and unknown, a stale reading is not working). No view calls it yet: the Worker's view and the dispatch (step 2), the card rows, group rows and "Who is on what" (step 3) and the truth check (step 4) follow, so nothing on the board changes with this step. The laws that need those views (the page names no evidence field, every open card in one crew column, a group's tile is the fold of its cards) come with them. New `tests/one-reading-module.test.mjs`.
