- With the week's quota used up, Manager is no longer woken once per idle agent's queue (#C1078; on 11-13 the quota hit 100% and Manager got a wake for each queued Boardsmith card, each one a turn that could change nothing). While the session's fresh reading of the week (the week_usage tool, written within the last hour for the week still running) is 98% or more, the idle-agent stall lines of the store are held: the "waits at <phase> 15 min", "idle since ..., N queued, next #C...", "no open session" and "nothing posted" lines are dropped from each round, and ONE line goes to Manager in their place: "The week's quota is used up (98% or more): idle-agent stall lines are held until it resets at <time>". The line holds the reset time and no percent, so a reading of 98, then 99, then 100 is one row for the whole spell. A stale or missing reading, or one under 98, holds nothing; after the reset the idle lines come back on their own. Held cards, open asks, prompts, landed packs and stale copies go on as before. New rule `quotaHold` in `worker/board-rules.mjs`, read by `syncStalls` in `worker/board-store.mjs`. No migration, table or column. Ships with a Worker deploy. Tests: `tests/dispatch-quiet-when-quota-out.test.mjs`.
