- A board tab left open holds less memory (board-memory-leak, #C1230; the owner: the board's Safari tab used 2 GB). Measured first on the live board for 7 minutes in a real browser: no listener, node or heap growth (nodes about 9.9k, heap floor 31 MB), so nothing leaked; what is big is the 5.5 MB view kept in the page and the screenshots, which the browser decodes at full size (40 pictures about 272 MB). Two changes, neither visible: a thumbnail whose picture has loaded and is wider than 720 px is drawn once into a small JPEG copy and shows that (`thumbSrc`, `makeThumb` in `page/board.template.html`; the lightbox still opens the full picture, `data-full`; the copies are capped at 300 and one still on the page is never dropped; a failed copy leaves the picture as it was), and the shim (`worker/compat-shim.mjs`) keeps a 53-bit hash and length of each collection instead of its whole JSON text. Same synthetic board before and after (about 5 MB, 10 pictures of 2400 x 1200, headless Chrome, heap read after a forced GC): heap 14.8 to 14.9 MB before, 11.4 to 11.6 MB after; nodes 112468 and listeners 633 before, 112468 and 634 after, flat over the run; decoded pictures 115 MB before, 8 MB after. Not measured: Safari. History is not dropped from the view (that changes what the board shows). Ships: a page change, so a pack with a hooks step. Tests: `tests/board-memory-flat.test.mjs`.
