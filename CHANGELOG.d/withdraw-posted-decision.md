- Manager can take back a decision it posted: Manager's own door has a new `withdraw_decision` tool (message_id). An unanswered decision loses its buttons and reads "No longer needed", and the card it held is released; it is refused once the owner has picked (his "Chose <key>: <label>" for one of its own options, before any later decision of the thread; his other messages and a pick of a newer decision do not count), on any other author's message and on a message that is no open decision (withdraw-posted-decision, #C1332). Ships: a Worker deploy (`worker/board-store.mjs`, `worker/board-worker.mjs`). Tests: `tests/withdraw-posted-decision.test.mjs` (mutation-checked).
