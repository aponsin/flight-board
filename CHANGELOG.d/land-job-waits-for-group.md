The land job (`scripts/board-land.mjs`, unchanged: it is one of the release tool's pinned entries) already waits for any hold and says `held: <card> (group)`, so a signed card waits until the last member is signed, and then the whole group is free in the same file and lands in one run (a chain lands as its tip). A card with no group, and one in a group of one, lands as before; a card's own hold (`dropped`, `paused`, `blocked`, `asked`, `waiting_on`) still names itself first.
