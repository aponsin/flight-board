- A card in a group (batch) never goes READY alone (batch-member-not-ready-alone, #C1021): on 11-12, in the group test g1-board-view, #C971 was built and #C969 of the same batch was not, no queued card depended on #C971, so `scripts/stack-ready.mjs` wrote the signal and #C971 went to review alone, breaking "one review, one pack". Now the stack queue file (`collector/store-dispatch.mjs`) carries each card's `batch` (a plain word, "" for none), `stack-ready` waits (`STACK-READY OK: ..., waiting for its group <batch> (<ids> not built): no signal`, no note) while another open card of the same batch and repository is not built, whoever builds it, and the dispatch counts such a card as a stack partner, so the built card gets no review turn until its peers are pushed (the hold lifts by itself then). A dependency wait still comes first; a card with no batch behaves as before; the lead pulls a card out of its batch by clearing its batch, and a dropped, paused or finished peer does not hold the group; a queue file older than 15 minutes or from before the batch word cannot make a card wait. Tests: `tests/batch-not-ready-alone.test.mjs` (mutation-checked: 12 guards across `scripts/stack-ready.mjs` and `collector/store-dispatch.mjs`, each failing a test). `collector/store-dispatch.mjs` is pinned code: it ships with an owner pack.
